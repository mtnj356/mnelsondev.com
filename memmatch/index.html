MemMatch

A classic memory matching game for Android.

Get it on Google Play Privacy policy

How to play

Tap a card to flip it, then tap another. If the numbers match, the pair stays face up. If they don't, both cards flip back, so remember where they were. Match every pair in as few turns and as little time as you can.

Features

  • Three board sizes: Easy (3×4), Normal (4×5), and Hard (5×6)
  • Tracks your fewest turns and best time for each size
  • Light and dark themes, portrait and landscape
  • No ads, no accounts, no tracking. MemMatch doesn't even use the internet.
