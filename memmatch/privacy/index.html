MemMatch Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

This policy describes how the MemMatch Android app (com.mnelsondev.memmatch), published by mnelsondev, handles your information.

The short version

MemMatch does not collect, transmit, or share any personal or sensitive information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no internet access.

Information stored on your device

MemMatch saves your best scores (fewest turns and fastest time for each difficulty) on your device so it can show them to you. This data never leaves your device through the app, and it is deleted when you uninstall MemMatch or clear its data.

Android backup

If you have turned on Android's backup feature, Android may include MemMatch's saved scores in your device backup with Google. That backup is managed by Android under your Google account settings, not by MemMatch, and the developer cannot access it.

Permissions

MemMatch does not request any permissions. It cannot access the internet, your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Sharing with third parties

MemMatch does not share any information with anyone, because it does not collect any. The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics code.

Children

MemMatch is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it does not collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to mnelsondev@gmail.com.
